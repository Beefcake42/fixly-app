Delete your Fixly account

The fastest way to delete your Fixly account is inside the app:

  1. Open Fixly.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

This deletes the Fixly account and associated repair and maintenance database records. Repair images stored for that account are also removed by the deletion process.

If you cannot access the app, publish a support contact method here before release so users can request deletion from outside the app.
