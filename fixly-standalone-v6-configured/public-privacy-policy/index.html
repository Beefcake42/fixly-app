Fixly Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Fixly is a repair assistance application designed to help user identify repair and maintenance problems, organize repairs, and recieve repair guidance.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Fixly, the app may collect information you provide directly, such as account information, repair information, descriptions of problems, and photographs you choose to upload for diagnosis.

Photos and Camera

Fixly may request access to your device's camera or photo library when you choose to photograph or upload an item to repair assistance. Images you submit may be processed to provide the requested diagnosis or repair guidance.

Account Information

If you create an account, Fixly may process information necessary to provide and maintain your account, such as your emailaddress and account-related information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to;

  • Provide Fixly's repair and diagnostic features.
  • Process photographs submitted for repair assistance.
  • Save repair information associated with your account.
  • Provide maintenance and reminder features.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the application.

Data Sharing

Fixly does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, or other technical service providers.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through Fixly. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Fixly's services and meet applications legal or operational requirements. Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data through Fixly's account-deletion process.

Children's Privacy

Fixly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Fixly's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
